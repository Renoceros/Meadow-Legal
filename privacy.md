# Privacy Policy for Meadow

**Effective Date:** June 8, 2026

Welcome to Meadow. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting it. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your information when you use our mobile application.

## 1. Information We Collect
To provide the core functionality of Meadow—a shared digital scrapbook for mothers and daughters—we collect the following types of data:
- **User Content:** Photos and captions that you actively upload to your shared timeline (Memory Lane).
- **Identifiers:** Anonymous session and pairing IDs (e.g., your 6-digit pairing code and auto-generated UUIDs). We do not require traditional accounts, emails, or phone numbers.

## 2. How We Use Your Data
The data we collect is used **solely** to operate the app and sync your private digital scrapbook between your device and your paired partner's device. We do not use your content for marketing, profiling, or any other unrelated purposes.

## 3. Data Processing and Third Parties
Your data is processed and securely stored using our cloud infrastructure provider, Supabase (which provides PostgreSQL database and Storage Buckets). 
- **No Sale of Data:** We **never** sell your data, photos, or identifiers to third parties.
- **Strict Privacy:** Your timeline is a strict 1-to-1 private network. No one other than your paired partner can view your uploaded photos or captions.

## 4. Your Control and Data Deletion
You have complete control over your data:
- **Delete Memories:** You can permanently delete any photo or caption you have uploaded at any time via the app's "Delete Memory" feature.
- **Unpair & Erase:** By using the "Unpair" feature, you instantly sever the link between you and your partner. You have the right to request full deletion of your session data and uploaded content from our servers.

## 5. Contact Us
If you have any questions or concerns about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at morenokristophan@icloud.com.
